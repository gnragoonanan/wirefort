# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists to be useful to people running VPN infrastructure. Be
decent to the people you interact with here.

**Expected:**

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people whose first language isn't
  yours or whose setup differs from yours.
- Critique the code, the configuration or the idea — not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most confusion comes from missing context, not malice.
- Accept that maintainers work on this in their spare time, and that "no" or
  "not now" are legitimate answers.

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal, political or identity-based attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Deliberately filing misleading reports, or using issues to advertise.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Security-specific expectations

Because this is security tooling, two extra rules apply:

- **Report vulnerabilities privately.** Do not post a working exploit against
  other people's deployments in a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Don't pressure people into unsafe advice.** If a maintainer declines to
  help you weaken a firewall policy or bypass authentication on infrastructure
  you haven't shown you own, accept it.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits and code review — and whenever someone is representing the project in
public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer,
[gnragoonanan (GnR)](https://github.com/gnragoonanan), via a GitHub private
message or a [security advisory](https://github.com/gnragoonanan/wirefort/security/advisories/new)
if the issue is sensitive.

Reports will be reviewed and handled as privately as the situation allows.
Depending on severity, the response may be a warning, removal of comments or
contributions, or a block from the repository. The maintainer's decision is
final. Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith can expect to
be called out on it.
